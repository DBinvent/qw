Sign in to this host, or pick a nickname to register.

Your passphrase is the key. It is stretched with Argon2id and used to unwrap the master key that signs your records and decrypts your account on this server. It is sent once over TLS to unlock the session and is never stored — pick something long, and reuse it nowhere.

Email is not a way back in. Registering takes a nickname and a passphrase. If an address is added it is stored encrypted at rest, the same as the nickname, and is only ever a verify / notify channel — never a passphrase reset. Forget the passphrase and the one-time recovery code shown right after you register is the only way back; without it the account is gone.

                    Every knob this client has. Each group opens for the full explanation. Some are wired; some are shown as not implemented so the surface matches what the mobile app will carry.

                    Inbound proposal filter
                    Two local pre-filters run on every inbound proposal before you look at it (abstract.md, "Basic Use Cases"). A proposal that fails one is flagged on the Contracts list — not hidden, and you can always admit it anyway. Both thresholds are private: a declined party is never told which one it failed, or that either is set. Leave a field blank to turn that check off.

                    Minimum reputation — your trust-path score for the requester in this skill area: the Quant value on the closing edge of the shortest verified credit-issuance path from you to them, halved per hop. No such path is unknown-risk, below any threshold.

                    Position limit — a multiplier of the requester's verified completed work in the last 90 days. 1.0 lets a proposal push your balance with them up to one times their recent output; 0 none.

                    Bureaus you trust NIP-QW15
                    A bureau reviews your skills against your countersigned work record and signs what it can stand behind (Profile tab → "Ask a bureau to review your skills"). List the ones you trust with a weight, read like any other node: >1 amplify, 1 neutral, <1 attenuate, 0 ignore. The Profile button uses the first entry by default.

                    Broadcast propagation NIP-QW14
                    A proposal / demand / profile / news / review you push travels hop to hop; each relaying node applies a per-type policy — how far it goes (hops), how old / large it may be (age, size), how wide each hop fans it out (fan-out), the minimum folded hop-score to keep relaying (score floor), how long this node's signed rating of an originator is reused (rating TTL), and a per-contact daily cap (rate). Stock NIP-QW14 §4 defaults until changed.

                    Reputation scoring not implemented NIP-QW13
                    The configurable per-domain scoring model (NIP-QW13 §1) — tolerance, rating / completion / quant weights, recency half-life, side_settled_factor (a contract with no credit issuance, NIP-QW01 kind 9006), mf2_weight (a direct contact's judgment of someone you distrust nudges their score, §2b), scope inheritance and the path knobs. Not built yet — qw_protocol::trust still uses the single-path magnitude score. Shown so the surface is visible.
                    Skill limits NIP-QW03
                    Enforced by the protocol on every profile: a skill tag is 1–79 characters and a profile carries fewer than 80 of them. Fixed, not editable.
